# Personal Workbench - Design System

## Product Identity
A personal command center for self-management. Think of a craftsman's well-organized workbench at dawn - every tool has its place, warm wood grain under soft morning light, the quiet confidence of knowing exactly what today holds.

## Visual Strategy
- Neumorphic soft UI with warm gray-white base
- Dual-layer shadows creating physical depth (cards feel like they float above the surface)
- Large rounded corners (24px) for a friendly, approachable feel
- Gradient accents in lavender purple for primary actions

## Color Palette
- Primary: #6C63FF (Lavender Purple) - focus, clarity
- Primary Gradient End: #896BFF
- Accent: #FF6584 (Coral Pink) - highlights, alerts
- Background/Surface: #F0F0F3 (Warm Gray White)
- Inset Surface: #E8E8EB (for inputs, recessed areas)
- Dark Shadow: #D1D9E6
- Light Shadow: #FFFFFF
- Text Primary: #2D3436
- Text Secondary: #636E72
- Success: #00B894
- Warning: #FDCB6E
- Danger: #FF6B6B

## Navigation Structure
5 Bottom Tabs:
1. Home (Dashboard overview + quick access)
2. Finance (Income/Expense tracking)
3. Schedule (Daily plans & tasks)
4. Goals (Goal management & progress)
5. Notes (Money insights, reviews, learning logs)

## Typography
- Page Title: 28px, weight 800
- Section Title: 18px, weight 700
- Card Title: 16px, weight 700
- Body: 14px, regular
- Caption: 12px, regular

## Design Don'ts
- No white (#FFFFFF) card backgrounds - use #F0F0F3
- No border-based separation - use shadows
- No rectangular selection indicators - use color + dot
- No single-layer shadows on iOS - always dual-layer nesting
